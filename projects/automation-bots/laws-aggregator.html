Legal Acts Bot

A Telegram bot that pushes newly published Moldovan legal acts to users, filtered by the topic sectors they subscribe to.

Stack
Python, aiogram
Workers
Celery + Redis
Data
PostgreSQL
Source
legis.md, polled every 30 min

Overview

Legal acts publish on legis.md constantly, but nobody wants to scan all the fields and check constantly even if no new legal act appears. I wanted people to pick a handful of sectors and get the matching acts pushed to them as they land, in Romanian, with a daily-digest option for anyone who would rather not be pinged in real time.

What I built

A second product on top of the event-aggregator’s stack. A scraper polls legis.md every 30 minutes, a matcher fans an incoming act out to every subscribed user, and a delivery worker sends the message and auto-pause the moment a user blocks the bot.

Highlights

  • Idempotent deliveries table so re-runs never double-send.
  • Fixture-tested parser with a 24h staleness alert if legis.md’s HTML shifts.
  • Romanian UI, inline sector keyboard, realtime ↔ daily-digest toggle.
