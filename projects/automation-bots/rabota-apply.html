Job Auto-Apply Bot

A scheduled Playwright bot that logs into rabota.md and applies once, per criteria, to every new software-engineer listing.

Stack
Python, Playwright, SQLite
Notifier
Telegram (apply, skip, failure)
Runtime
systemd timer on a Hetzner VPS

Overview

New rabota.md postings are worth a look, but never a full sitting. I wanted a bot that opened each one, decided whether it matched my keywords and salary rule, and sent my CV — never twice for the same job, and never silently.

What I built

Six small units: config, an auth module with cookie-cached login, a read-only scraper, a matcher (keyword + salary + email-only filter), an applier that drives the “Trimite CV” modal end to end (“CV DE PE SITE” → consent → submit), and a SQLite store keyed by the site’s stable job id for dedup. A Telegram notifier posts every apply, every skip with a reason, and every failure. Runs on a systemd timer on a Hetzner VPS.

Highlights

  • Dry-run mode, per-run apply cap.
  • Detects “email your CV to…” listings and skips them rather than force-applying.
  • Idempotent dedup keyed by the site’s own numeric job id, so a re-run never re-applies.
